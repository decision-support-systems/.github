# Housing & AI

A central space for [University of Huddersfield](https://hud.ac.uk) developers to collaborate on software, research, and deployment related to Housing and Artificial Intelligence projects.

This organisation helps teams keep code, documentation, and project resources in one place — supporting easier collaboration between internal developers, researchers, and supervisors.

---

## 🔧 What You'll Find Here
- **AI & Machine Learning software**
- **Research tools and prototypes**
- **Internal documentation**
- **Utilities for deployment and maintenance**

Repositories may be **public or private** depending on project requirements.

---

## 👥 Who This Organisation Is For
This organisation is intended for:
- University of Huddersfield developers  
- Staff and researchers working on Housing & AI aligned projects  
- Internal collaboration teams  

External collaborators may be added on a project-by-project basis.

---

## 🤝 Collaboration & Contribution
We use a **minimal governance approach**:

- Contributors are added by the maintainers.
- Projects may use their own branch or PR rules.
- Please follow the documentation provided within each repository.

If you require access to a project, contact one of the maintainers listed below.
