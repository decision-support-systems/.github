# Decision Support Systems

A central space for [University of Huddersfield](https://hud.ac.uk) researchers and developers to collaborate on software, research, and deployment related to AI-enabled decision support systems.

This organisation brings together domain-focused projects, research outputs, and technical prototypes — covering areas such as housing strategy, health care, and operational analytics — developed with public and private sector partners.

The showcase site is live at **[decision-support-systems.github.io](https://decision-support-systems.github.io)**.

---

## 🔧 What You'll Find Here
- **Domain-led decision support projects and prototypes**
- **Machine learning, generative AI, and agentic workflow tools**
- **Research publications and implementation evidence**
- **Data analysis, informatics, and visualisation work**
- **Internal documentation and deployment utilities**

Repositories may be **public or private** depending on project requirements.

---

## 🧭 Decision Domains
Work in this organisation is structured around applied decision support domains:
- **Housing** — retrofit planning, asset prioritisation, sustainable strategy
- **Health Care** — triage support, care pathway optimisation, risk-informed decisions
- Additional domains onboarded as partnerships develop

---

## 👥 Who This Organisation Is For
This organisation is intended for:
- University of Huddersfield researchers and developers
- Staff working on decision support, applied AI, or data analytics projects
- Postdoctoral researchers and research technicians within the hub
- Internal collaboration teams and project supervisors

External collaborators may be added on a project-by-project basis.

---

## 🤝 Collaboration & Contribution
We use a **minimal governance approach**:

- Contributors are added by the maintainers.
- Projects may use their own branch or PR rules.
- Please follow the documentation provided within each repository.

If you require access to a project, contact one of the maintainers listed below.
